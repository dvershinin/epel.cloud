epel.cloud is an independent, Cloudflare-cached mirror of the EPEL repositories, plus a cookbook of tested Enterprise Linux recipes. It is not an official Fedora or Red Hat service. Packages and metadata come from Fedora mirrors unchanged.

Run by GetPageSpeed, packagers of NGINX modules and server software for Enterprise Linux. Mirror content follows upstream licensing; recipes are tested on the dates stated and may age.
